Bring your own road.

Every output video frame receives a mask. The larger models run on keyframes; optical flow tracks the mask between them.

Optional GPS track CSV or GPX

GPS locates the camera route; depth still estimates the road width. Upload a timed CSV with timestamp_s,latitude,longitude and optional accuracy_m, or a timed GPX track.

CSV times are seconds from GPS start. GPX is aligned from its first timed point. Use this offset to synchronize.
Measured camera calibration Optional

Supply calibrated intrinsics, height and pose to enable the stricter ground-plane estimator. Leave empty to use rough learned depth.

Example values are not a calibration for your camera.
◈ Unknown camera? Rough metres use outdoor learned depth and the FOV assumption. The range is illustrative uncertainty, not a validated confidence interval. Without GPS, the map uses approximate visual odometry and may drift.
UNDER THE SURFACE

Perception, depth, motion.

SegFormer B2 runs on aspect-preserved frames. Outdoor Depth Anything V2 supplies a rough metric-depth prior. Optical flow tracks masks between fresh model inferences.

01 / WidthRoad-edge spans plus depth and a camera FOV assumption give rough metre estimates and broad sensitivity ranges. Calibrated measurements remain separate.
02 / VideoFull-duration H.264 video with per-frame overlays, plus dense inspection snapshots. Mask tracking is not independent model inference.
03 / MapGPS-aligned positions or depth-scaled visual odometry accumulate an observed corridor with projected roadside context. Gaps are omitted; unseen landscape is not invented.

The width strip covers all analyzed timestamps even if spatial tracking fails. Its vertical axis is time, not distance. Neither learned depth nor a smooth mask establishes surveying accuracy.

Video and optional GPS remain on this local server. Full normalized video, rendered video, frames and results are retained in the data folder. Model weights download once. Depth and segmentation remain sensitive to unfamiliar environments.
